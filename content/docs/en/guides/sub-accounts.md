# Sub-accounts

A sub-account is a separate WABEK account, with its own dashboard and sign-in, that you create and manage from your account. It suits agencies serving several clients, or businesses with branches.

## Team member vs. sub-account

| | Team member | Sub-account |
|---|---|---|
| Data | Works on your account's data | Separate, isolated data |
| Numbers | Uses your numbers | Connects its own numbers |
| Sign-in | Invited into your account | Its own email and password |

> **Note:** This feature requires a plan that includes **Sub-Accounts**, and only the account owner can manage it.

## Create a sub-account

1. Open **Sub-Accounts** and click create.
2. Fill in the fields:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Full Name | The client's or branch's name |
| Email | Sign-in email (must not already be registered) |
| Password | At least 8 characters |
| Max Instances | Allocated numbers (default 1) |
| Max Contacts | Allocated contacts (`-1` for unlimited) |
| Max Messages/Month | Allocated monthly messages (default 1,000) |

3. Save, then send the sign-in details to the sub-account's owner securely.

The sub-account gets its own API key, and its data never mixes with yours or with other sub-accounts.

## Managing

From the same page you can change a sub-account's limits or disable it. Disabling signs the account out and blocks sign-in without deleting its data.

> **Warning:** Ask the sub-account's owner to change the password after the first sign-in.
