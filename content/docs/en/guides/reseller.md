# Reseller program

The reseller program lets you resell WABEK plans to your clients at prices you set, and track clients and earnings from the **Reseller** page.

> **Note:** This feature requires a plan that includes **Reseller**, and your reseller account may need approval.

## 1. Create a custom plan

1. Open the **Plans & Pricing** tab and click **Create Custom Plan**.
2. Fill in the fields:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Base Plan | The underlying plan you pay the platform for |
| Plan Name | The name your clients see |
| Your Price / Month | Your monthly price |
| Your Price / Year | Your yearly price (optional) |

3. Save.

## 2. Create an invite link

1. Open the **Invite Links** tab and click **Create Invite Link**.
2. Fill in the fields:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Label | Internal name for the link |
| Custom Code | A custom code (optional) |
| Plan | The custom plan attached to the invite |
| Free Trial Days | Free trial length |
| Discount % | Discount percentage |
| Max Uses | Maximum uses (empty = unlimited) |

3. Copy and share the link. Clients who sign up through it are linked to you as their reseller.

## 3. Payment method

In the **Settings** tab, choose how your clients pay:

| Option | Description |
|---|---|
| Through WABEK platform | Clients pay the platform, and your earnings are paid out each payout cycle |
| My own Stripe account | Clients pay your Stripe account directly, after you connect it with OAuth |
| Both options | Both at once |

To connect Stripe, click the connect button and follow Stripe's steps. WABEK never sees your Stripe password or keys, and you can disconnect at any time.

## 4. Payouts

In **Settings → Payout Settings**, set the payout schedule, method (bank transfer or PayPal) and account details. Payment history is in the **Payouts** tab.

## Tracking

| Tab | Content |
|---|---|
| Overview | Summary of clients and earnings, and gift code redemption |
| Referrals | Clients who signed up through your links |
| Campaigns | Send a campaign to your clients |

> **Warning:** You are responsible to your clients for the prices and invoices you set, especially when using your own Stripe account.
