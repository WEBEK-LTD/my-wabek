# Billing

The **Billing** page shows your current plan, its limits and renewal date, and lets you upgrade and manage your subscription.

## Subscribe or upgrade

1. Open **Billing**.
2. Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly** (yearly appears when plans offer it).
3. Click the plan you want. You are taken to a secure Stripe checkout page.
4. Complete payment. You return to WABEK and see an activation confirmation.

Prices depend on the plan and cycle, and are shown on the page before you pay.

> **Note:** If you signed up through a reseller link, you may see the reseller's plans and prices, and payment may go to the reseller directly.

## Manage your subscription

Click the manage-subscription button to open the Stripe portal, where you can update your card, download invoices and cancel.

## Gift codes

1. Click **Have a gift code? Redeem here**.
2. Enter the code and click **Redeem**.
3. The plan attached to the code is activated for the code's duration.

## When a subscription ends

Your account and data are not deleted. The Free plan's limits and features apply until you renew. See [Plans and limits](/docs/getting-started/plans-limits).

> **Note:** Meta's fees for Cloud API messages (if any) are separate from your WABEK subscription and are paid to Meta through your WhatsApp Business Account.
