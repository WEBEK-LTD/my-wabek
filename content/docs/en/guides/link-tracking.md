# Link tracking

**Link Tracker** creates a short link that redirects visitors to your original URL and records every click.

## Create a link

1. Open **Link Tracker** and click create.
2. Enter the **original URL**. It must start with `http://` or `https://`.
3. Enter a descriptive title (optional) and link it to a campaign (optional).
4. Save. The short link looks like:

```
https://waabek.com/l/Ab3xK9
```

5. Copy the short link and put it in your message or campaign.

## Reading results

The page shows the click count for each link. Each click is recorded with its time, the visitor's browser and IP address.

## Rules

- Invalid or deleted links redirect visitors to the home page.
- Only `http` and `https` URLs are accepted.
- How many links you can create depends on your plan.

> **Note:** IP addresses and browser details are personal data under some laws. Mention click tracking in your business's privacy policy.
